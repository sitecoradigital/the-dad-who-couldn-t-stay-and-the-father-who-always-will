THE STORY

For the questions little hearts carry

The Dad Who Couldn't Stay And The Father Who Always Will is a heartfelt children's book for boys and girls growing up without their earthly father. Through tender conversations between a child and their mother, it offers reassurance that a father's absence is not a child's fault and that God's love remains present.

Written to encourage families to talk with care, the book shares a message of comfort, belonging, and hope.
